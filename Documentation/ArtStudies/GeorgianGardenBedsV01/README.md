# Georgian mixed beds V01 — art direction and lineage

The two plan images are original generated studies for complete garden pieces, not single plants. The square uses a 4 × 4 m footprint; the broad rectangle uses 6 × 3 m. The compositions follow a restrained, bilateral 18th-century-inspired planting rhythm: straight contained edge, dark cultivated soil, compact clipped shrubs, and many small cream-white and dusty-rose flower masses across the whole bed. Botanical identity is deliberately generic; no species-specific seasonal claim is implied.

`square-summer.png` was generated from a square, straight-down orthographic prompt specifying photographic planting texture, multiple shrub and flower groups throughout the area, dark soil, and a narrow pale edge within the square. `rectangle-summer.png` used the same direction in a broad rectangular arrangement. `square-winter.png` and `rectangle-winter.png` were image edits of their respective summer plans, preserving bed boundaries and arrangement while replacing open flowers with dormant stems and muted shrubs. `square-isometric-study.png` re-rendered the square plan as a separate visual direction study at a requested shallow game angle; it is not a runtime billboard because a single pre-rendered angle would fail when rotated.

The four plan PNGs under `Assets/CityForgeV3/Resources/CityForgeV3/Garden/GeorgianBedsV01/` are byte-identical copies of the source files here. Unity import disables NPOT rescaling to retain detail. The existing versioned GeorgianBorderV01 upright flower and thicket cutouts are reused for local depth; those source files are not modified. The runtime assembly projects the overhead art on the ground and places the upright cards and physical edging in 3D, so the Lot camera computes the isometric projection and rotation.
