# Running water for the stone garden fountain

The existing `stone-garden-fountain-v01` Garden prop now builds presentation-only water under its grounded model. The supplied FBX, albedo and saved ID are unchanged. The water is a child of the prop root, so placement, quarter-turn rotation, selection, Undo and manual-only Save continue through the existing prop path. A live fountain retained through Unity's script reload reattaches the water once; no Lot presentation rebuild is needed.

The imported model was measured at the normalized three-metre diameter. Its lower rim is around 0.5 m, middle rim around 1.9 m, and top rim around 2.8 m. Three inset water disks sit below those rims. One combined mesh carries eight upper and ten lower thin crossed-ribbon streams, from the top bowl into the middle bowl and from there into the basin. A small transparent shader scrolls highlights down the streams and moves muted ripples across the water disks. Two low-rate particle systems add tiny impact droplets at the middle and lower water levels. Shared meshes and materials avoid rebuilding geometry for each placed fountain; per-instance property blocks carry preview opacity. The effect adds no collider, fluid simulation, saved state, or sound.

Validation: `Validation/stone-fountain-water-v01/` contains a close isolated view, two different shader phases, a half-opacity placement preview, and a render from the active Lot Camera. The active live fountain was confirmed to have one water assembly with four mesh renderers and two particle systems. The Editor was paused during review and remained paused. The two captured shader phases differ across 23,407 water-region pixels, confirming visible shader motion when time advances; continuous movement in the resumed Game view has not yet been observed. Unity compiled with zero assembly errors. The temporary QA script and bridge hook were removed. No Save was called, and the active Lot session was not modified. The focused EditMode assertions were updated but not run while the Editor remained in Play mode. Previous forest and regional suites do not validate this Garden effect.

Joel then spotted that the flowing highlights traveled upward. The stream mesh assigns UV.y = 0 at the upper lip and UV.y = 1 at the lower bowl, so its phase now subtracts time: `sin(38 * uv.y - 13 * time)`. A moving highlight therefore advances toward increasing UV.y at 13/38 UV units per second. The basin ripple's outward phase was already correct. Unity refreshed with zero assembly errors; the paused Editor and active Lot remained untouched. The phase screenshots above predate this one-line direction correction and should not be used to judge direction.
