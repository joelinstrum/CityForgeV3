# Forest family mix V01 artwork lineage

Date: September 19, 2026

Destination: `Assets/CityForgeV3/Resources/CityForgeV3/Flora/ForestClustersFamilyMixV01/`

Fourteen new 1254×1254 RGBA PNGs were generated with OpenAI's built-in image
generation tool. The existing approved realistic forest cluster was supplied as
a style reference. No legacy City Forge UI, prefab, style, or layout was copied.
The destination files are new canonical runtime assets; older forest cluster art
remains untouched for saved-ID compatibility.

The compact source prompts requested one flattened transparent billboard with a
narrow shared root line and no ground, shadow, text, or watermark:

- Deciduous dominant: four deciduous trees plus one fir.
- Fir & Mountain dominant: four varied fir/spruce trees plus one deciduous tree.
- Tropical: two fan palms, one date palm, and two tropical broadleaf trees.

The large prompts used the same contract with nine depicted trees:

- Deciduous dominant: eight deciduous trees plus one fir.
- Fir & Mountain dominant: eight fir/spruce trees plus one deciduous tree.
- Tropical: three fan palms, two date palms, and four tropical broadleaf trees.

Seasonal edits kept composition and silhouette fixed. Autumn changed deciduous
foliage only; winter removed deciduous foliage only, retained bare branches, and
kept firs evergreen without snow. Tropical art is intentionally season-neutral.

Selected generation outputs were copied without pixel edits. Their original
tool-output filenames and exact runtime mapping are preserved in
`Documentation/Validation/forest-family-mix-v01/prompts-and-lineage.md`.
