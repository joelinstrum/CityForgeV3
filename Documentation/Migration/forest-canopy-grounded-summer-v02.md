# Grounded summer deciduous canopy experiment v02

The two RGBA cutouts in `Assets/CityForgeV3/Resources/CityForgeV3/Flora/ForestCanopyGroundedSummerV02/` are non-destructive edits of the v01 large and compact summer canopies. They preserve the high-oblique crown compositions, colors, and transparent background while revealing four to six trunks in the large cluster and three to four in the compact cluster. The lower foliage is sparser so the trunks meet the district ground. Both are 1536 × 1024 with alpha; the v01 sources remain untouched.

The district forest resource preference selects v02 for spring and summer deciduous compact/large clusters at every zoom. Autumn retains the v01 canopy, winter its prior leafless artwork, and other flora are unchanged. The existing instance identity, footprint, pivot, scale, terrain anchoring, shadow, selection, and save data are unchanged. To revert the test, return the spring/summer resource preference to `ForestCanopyFarV01`; the original assets are still present.

Visual QA was captured in an isolated Unity 6.1 EditMode fixture with a synthetic 168-cluster district at LOD1 and LOD4. See `Documentation/Previews/forest-canopy-grounded-summer-v02-close.png` and `Documentation/Previews/forest-canopy-grounded-summer-v02-region.png`. The fixture's regular placement grid is not production forest generation. Joe's live editor was not driven. The 31 forest/flora tests passed, including summer and autumn resource selection at near and far zoom. A separate dense 384-cluster season test retained 32 mesh batches and 97 bounded slices (maximum 14.28 ms, p95 13.32 ms in the final headless run); this short run does not establish gameplay frame-time, GPU draw calls, or long-duration stability. The change does not add per-frame work or an extra district traversal.

Built-in imagegen edited each v01 summer PNG separately with this prompt intent: preserve the existing crown silhouette, high-oblique camera, muted olive-green color, lighting, and alpha; reveal a few natural dark-brown trunks and branching stems at varied depths, extending to a shared ground-contact line near the canvas bottom; thin only the lowest foliage; no terrain, shadow, halo, sky, or text. No CLI/API generator was used.
