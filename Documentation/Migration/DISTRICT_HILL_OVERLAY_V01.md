# Hill surface overlay — first blending trial

Joe accepted the finer v02 generated short-grass/earth decal and requested in-game placement. Source `CityForgeMCP/artifacts/terrain/hill-overlay-v02/dry-grass-earth-v02.png` is copied unchanged to `CityForgeV3/Decals/HillsV01/dry-grass-earth-v02`. RGBA1254 square, real alpha, mipmaps/trilinear/aniso4, clamp wrapping, alpha-edge color dilation. Source and installed SHA256 match in texture-audit.json.

`DistrictGroundDecals` creates a separate `DistrictHillGroundOverlay` on hilly districts. Patch positions and rotations are deterministic from district ID and hill seed; no resource deposits, flora records or height settings are added. Quad grids follow the central terrain sampler. Placement excludes river channels/banks; level infrastructure buffers and low terrain fade out. Slope and varied patch coverage modulate opacity, capped at .20. Nominal spacing14m, position jitter13m, width8–12m; subdivided into4x4 cells and batched in chunks. Meshes/material clean up with their owner.

The dedicated HillGroundOverlay shader retains the accepted terrain illumination/relief calculation, uses the current ground material's sunlight direction, ambient floor, relief strength and tint, and blends via original texture alpha plus vertex/edge fade. No per-tree brightness override, base-grass replacement, hill geometry or sun change. Existing default leaf decals are unchanged.

The .32 opacity/small-jitter first trial was rejected during district-scale inspection as too speckled. Final .20 opacity, wider jitter and .28 edge feather are subtler. Future texture variants may reduce remaining recognizable shapes. This first trial awaits Joe's visual acceptance.
