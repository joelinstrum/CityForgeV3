# 18th Century Ladies Chatting V01

The only colonial lady source found was `/Users/joelinstrum/Downloads/3d characters/colonial-lady/colonial-lady-red-dress.zip`, with its preview PNG. Both were copied byte-for-byte to `SourceArchives/ColonialLadiesV01/`; hashes are in `colonial-ladies-chat-v01-source-manifest.json`. The source FBX has one 1,183-vertex rigged mesh, 41 bones, and idle and walk actions. Its authored height is 0.967 units and is calibrated to 1.72 m for this clip. The preview is a reference, not a runtime asset.

`Tools/build_colonial_ladies_chat_atlases.py` duplicates the source rig in Blender, places the two women facing one another, and bakes two differentiated performances from eight directions. The red lady keeps her hands at her hips. The blue lady folds her arms, briefly drops them, then folds them again; her underlying idle action is offset from the red lady’s. Both lower bodies are held in the source standing pose so the conversation does not turn into a synchronized step. A shader mask changes the second dress from crimson to muted blue while preserving the face, apron, bonnet, hair, and shoes. The canonical texture remains untouched. `Tools/pack_colonial_ladies_chat_atlases.py` packs 16 frames per direction at 2 fps (an 8-second loop) into eight 1792 × 256 atlases. Those atlases and a thumbnail are the only runtime art, under `CityForgeV3/Automata/ColonialLadiesChatV01/Group/`.

The Automata catalog registers **18th Century Ladies Chatting** as one 4 × 4 m selectable, draggable group. The shared `AutomataClipPlayer` presents the whole pair as one sprite renderer, with its existing yellow selection outline, rotation, visibility checkboxes, serialization, and undo. No per-lady runtime script or skeleton is added. Its default schedule is morning, noon, and afternoon in all seasons; placements can change that in the inspector. No automatic disk save is added.

The source includes only one lady model. The blue lady is a colored derivative of the same model; a separately authored character could replace her in a later art pass without changing the runtime clip contract.
