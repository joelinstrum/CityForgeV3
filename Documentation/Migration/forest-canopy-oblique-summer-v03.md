# Oblique summer deciduous canopy experiment v03

The two RGBA cutouts in `Assets/CityForgeV3/Resources/CityForgeV3/Flora/ForestCanopyObliqueSummerV03/` are non-destructive imagegen edits of the v02 compact and large summer canopies. The user's SimCity 4 screenshot was a reference for front-to-back planting and trunk-base heights only; no SimCity artwork, terrain, shadows, or UI was copied. The new artwork retains City Forge's detailed crowns and muted palette, shows more canopy tops, places rear trunks higher and partly behind foreground foliage, and gives nearer trunks uneven root-flared bases. Both final assets are 1536 × 1024 RGBA cutouts with true alpha. V02 and older sources remain intact.

Spring and summer deciduous compact/large clusters now select v03 at every district zoom. Autumn retains v01; winter and other flora are unchanged. Instance IDs, footprints, pivot, scale, selection, simulation, save data, and the projected-shadow contract are unchanged. Reverting the trial is one resource-path preference change. Each cluster remains a single camera-facing billboard anchored at one district point: the art improves depth cues, but it cannot put every depicted trunk at an independent 3D terrain coordinate. A true multi-trunk spatial composition would be a separate, costlier design.

The intricate v03 alpha edge made Unity's default tight sprite geometry expensive: in the isolated fixture, compact v02 had 463 vertices and compact v03 had 708; large v02 had 468 and large v03 had 1,495. Only v03 therefore uses a four-vertex full-rect sprite with the existing alpha-cutout shader. This preserves the visible silhouette and local flora batching while exchanging triangle cost for transparent-pixel overdraw. A 120-cluster visual fixture was rendered at LOD0, LOD1, and LOD4 and checked for edge differences. The resulting images are `Documentation/Previews/forest-canopy-oblique-summer-v03-detail.png`, `...-close.png`, and `...-region.png`. The fixture's regular tree placement is not production forest generation. Joe's live editor was not driven.

All 31 forest/flora EditMode tests passed in isolated Unity 6.1, including a transition back to the four-vertex summer sprite. In the 384-cluster dense transition, summer and autumn still used 32 flora mesh batches, and the season change took 97 four-cluster slices; final headless maximum was 13.89 ms and p95 was 11.54 ms. Before the quad switch, the same fixture measured 28.68 ms maximum and 22.08 ms p95. These short CPU measurements do not establish actual GPU overdraw, draw calls, full-frame spikes, or long-duration stability in Joe's district.

Built-in imagegen was used separately for the large and compact edits. Prompt intent: preserve each v02 canopy's natural high-quality foliage, colors, and lighting while shifting the render toward a high-oblique/isometric view with staggered rear/near trunks, partially occluded rear bases, irregular rooted lower silhouette, true transparency, and no ground, cast shadow, halo, or text. No CLI/API image generator was used.
